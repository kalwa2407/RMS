Google Play Data Safety Policy

Account & Data Deletion

Users of the Persian Darbar application (in.persiandarbar.customer) can request complete deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

Option 1: In-App Deletion

Open the Persian Darbar app on your Android device → Navigate to My Profile → Scroll down to Account Management → Tap Delete Account.

Option 2: Direct Web / Email Request

Click below to dispatch an automated deletion request to our privacy desk:

✉️ Submit Deletion Request via Email
Data Removal Details:
  • Google Authentication credentials and Firebase user ID are permanently revoked.
  • Saved home/work delivery addresses and telephone numbers are purged within 30 days.
  • Financial invoices are retained in anonymized format solely for legal GST compliance.
