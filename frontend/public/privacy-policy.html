📌 Policy Overview & Entity Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Persian Darbar ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information through the Persian Darbar mobile application (Google Play Package: in.persiandarbar.customer), the Persian Darbar Staff mobile application (Google Play Package: in.persiandarbar.staff), and the official website at https://persiandarbar.in.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary data required to deliver high quality dining services, food ordering, reservations, and restaurant staff management.

📱 Customer App (in.persiandarbar.customer)

  • Personal Identity: Full Name, Email Address, and Avatar via Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication).
  • Contact Details: Verified phone number for order updates and OTP delivery.
  • Delivery Addresses: Saved physical street address and landmarks for home delivery fulfillment.
  • Orders & Bookings: Food items ordered, billing amounts, order timestamps, table reservation details.
  • Location Data (Approximate/Precise): Temporary front-end device coordinates only when clicking "Detect Current Location" to calculate delivery radius. We do NOT track location in the background.

👥 Staff App (in.persiandarbar.staff)

  • Staff Authentication: Role identification (Admin, Captain, Kitchen, Delivery) and secure login credentials.
  • Restaurant Operations: Dine-in table orders, live Kitchen Display System (KDS) order statuses, inventory updates, and delivery dispatch timestamps.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Operating system version and network connection status for internal offline/online sync stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Food Ordering & Table Reservations: Processing your dining and delivery orders and reserving tables at Persian Darbar.
  • Account Authentication: Authenticating user profiles securely via Google Firebase Auth without needing passwords.
  • Real-Time Updates: Sending transactional notifications and WhatsApp confirmations regarding table booking approvals and live delivery progress.
  • Payment Processing: Reconciling payments through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.
  • Customer Support: Assisting you through staff phone assistance or our automated chat assistant.
  • Security & Compliance: Preventing fraudulent orders and maintaining audit records compliant with Indian law.

3. Third-Party Services & SDK Disclosures

We never sell or rent your personal information to third-party data brokers or advertisers. We share data only with the following trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud Firestore database, user authentication, and push messaging. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Google Cloud Platform (Google LLC): Backend API hosting (Cloud Run in Mumbai / asia-south1 region, ensuring data residency in India).
  • Meta WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta Platforms, Inc.): Automated delivery of transactional reservation confirmations and dispatch notifications.
  • Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd.: Secure payment gateway. Persian Darbar does not collect, view, or store payment card numbers, CVVs, or bank net banking credentials.
  • OpenAI (OpenAI LLC): Natural language queries for automated customer support chat.

4. Device Permissions & Location Transparency

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to access online menus, cloud order synchronization, and live reservation tracking.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify internet connectivity and notify users when offline.
  • Location Permission: Optional. Requested only within the browser / webview when a customer chooses "Detect Location" to compute distance from Persian Darbar, Pune. Location is not polled in the background or shared with third-party advertisers.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Data Transit Security: All network requests are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3 over HTTPS.
  • Data at Rest: Database records are encrypted at rest with AES-256 standard on Google Firebase and Google Cloud servers.
  • Data Retention: Account data is retained as long as your profile remains active. Transactional billing records and tax receipts are maintained in compliance with statutory Indian business and GST legislation.

🗑️ 6. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Policy Compliance)

In strict adherence to Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users of the Persian Darbar app have the absolute right to request the deletion of their account and all associated personal data, both in-app and via this web link, without having to maintain the app installed on their phone.

Method 1: Inside the Customer App

1. Open the Persian Darbar App.
2. Go to My Profile → Account Management.
3. Tap "Delete Account" and confirm.

Method 2: Web & Email Request

Submit an account deletion request directly by sending an email with your registered phone number or email address:

✉️ Email Deletion Request
What data is wiped:

Upon receiving your deletion request, your Google Authentication identity, stored profile credentials, delivery addresses, and phone records are permanently erased within 30 days. Lawful tax transaction receipts are retained only as mandated by Indian GST accounting rules.

7. Children's Privacy (Google Families Policy)

Our applications are intended for adults and general diners aged 13 and above (and 18+ for staff personnel). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child under 13 has submitted personal data to us, please notify us at privacy@persiandarbar.in and we will delete the data immediately.
